Green Goddess Grain Bowl

Herby, crunchy, and filling—an easy lunch that makes the most of fresh greens.

You'll need

  • 2 cups cooked quinoa
  • 1 ripe avocado and 1 cucumber
  • 2 cups leafy greens
  • ¼ cup plain yogurt, lemon, and fresh herbs

Make it

  1. Whisk yogurt with lemon juice and chopped herbs.
  2. Divide quinoa, greens, cucumber, and avocado between bowls.
  3. Spoon the dressing over top and season to taste.

Weeknight favorite25 min · Serves 4

One-Pan Tomato Pasta

A cozy, low-fuss dinner with sweet tomatoes, garlic, and a little parmesan.

You'll need

  • 12 oz pasta
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves and 3½ cups vegetable broth
  • Olive oil, parmesan, and basil

Make it

  1. Add pasta, tomatoes, sliced garlic, broth, and a drizzle of oil to a deep pan.
  2. Bring to a boil, then simmer, stirring often, until pasta is tender.
  3. Finish with parmesan, torn basil, and black pepper.

Golden Carrot & Ginger Soup

Silky, gently spiced soup that brings a bit of sunshine to any day.

You'll need

  • 1½ lb carrots, peeled and chopped
  • 1 onion and a 1-inch piece of fresh ginger
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • ½ cup coconut milk, salt, and pepper

Make it

  1. Soften chopped onion in a pot with a little oil, then add grated ginger.
  2. Add carrots and broth. Simmer until the carrots are very tender.
  3. Blend until smooth, stir in coconut milk, and season to taste.
